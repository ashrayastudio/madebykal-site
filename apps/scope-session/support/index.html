Please do not send passwords, payment details, full backups or private photos unless they are needed to resolve the problem.

Restore a purchase

Use Restore Purchases in Settings with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Restoring a purchase restores logging access, not local imaging records.

Recover your records

Use Settings → Restore Backup from Files to select a complete Scope Session backup. Review the counts before replacing your library. CSV and PDF reports are readable exports; they do not restore the complete library.

If your library will not open

Unlock your iPhone and try again. Keep the app installed and contact support if the problem continues. Removing the app may remove its local records.

Privacy and security

For a privacy request, email privacy@madebykal.com. Report security issues to security@madebykal.com.
